How It Works (Beta Version)

1. Connect Wallet

Use MetaMask or WalletConnect to link your non-custodial wallet to the app.

2. Get Virtual Card

We issue a virtual card tied to your wallet and off-ramp partner. Add it to Google Pay.

3. Tap to Pay

Use your phone like a card. Spend crypto instantly at any NFC terminal that supports Google Pay.

What’s Coming Next?
